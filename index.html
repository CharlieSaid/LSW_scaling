Lego Star Wars scaling tool: Compare scales against canon ship sizes.

Created by BAUFman Bricks and Charlie Said

Official Lego scales are inconsistent across themes and eras. We plot measured Lego sizes against movie/book canon dimensions so builders can see how UCS, Playscale, and BAUFman-scale sets actually compare — and convert sizes with curves fit to real data, not a single fixed ratio.
